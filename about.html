About Us

Broad Ripple Capital, Inc. is a privately held commercial real estate investment and development company headquartered in Indianapolis, Indiana. We specialize in identifying and acquiring high-traffic, high-visibility highway commercial sites that present unique opportunities for long-term growth and community impact.

Our core development strategy targets sites ideal for mixed-use grocery-anchored centers as well as outlot development opportunities for national and regional brands. We actively pursue properties that serve growing suburban corridors and emerging residential hubs across Indiana and the Midwest.

Our team has decades of combined experience in site acquisition, entitlement, leasing, construction, and asset management. We work closely with national and regional tenants, brokers, municipalities, and investors to deliver quality developments that enhance communities and generate strong returns.

Investment Criteria

Broad Ripple Capital targets assets that align with our long-term growth strategy and deliver strong value to tenants and surrounding communities. Our investment criteria include:

  • Location: High-traffic corridors, major intersections, and regional trade areas with visibility from highways or primary arterials.
  • Demographics: Strong population growth, high daytime traffic, and income levels that support national retail and service providers.
  • Size & Scale: Sites typically ranging from 1 to 10 acres, suitable for grocery-anchored centers, retail strip centers, or pad users.
  • Zoning & Entitlements: Preference for sites zoned commercial or with a clear path to entitlement for commercial or mixed-use development.

Site Selection Strategy

We utilize a data-driven and relationship-based approach to identify development-ready sites with strong fundamentals. Our strategy includes:

  • Working closely with municipalities to align with growth and redevelopment goals.
  • Building relationships with national brokers, retailers, and tenants to understand current market demand.
  • Evaluating traffic patterns, surrounding land uses, competitive landscape, and accessibility to determine development feasibility.
  • Prioritizing locations with potential for multi-tenant synergy, long-term leasing stability, and community impact.
