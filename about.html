About Us

Broad Ripple Capital, Inc. is a privately held commercial real estate investment and development company headquartered in Indianapolis, Indiana. We specialize in identifying high-potential real estate opportunities throughout the Midwest with a primary focus on highway commercial, mixed-use, and infill redevelopment projects.

Our team has decades of combined experience in site acquisition, entitlement, leasing, construction, and asset management. We work closely with national and regional tenants, brokers, municipalities, and investors to deliver quality developments that enhance communities and generate strong returns.

What We Do

  • Commercial site selection and land acquisition
  • Ground-up retail and mixed-use development
  • Build-to-suit and pre-leasing for national tenants
  • Investment property acquisition and repositioning

Our Mission

We strive to build lasting value through thoughtful real estate development that meets the needs of businesses and communities alike.

Core Values

  • Integrity: We believe in doing the right thing — always.
  • Excellence: We deliver high-quality developments through careful planning and execution.
  • Partnership: We build strong relationships with tenants, investors, and local stakeholders.
  • Vision: We identify opportunities others overlook and turn them into reality.
